<reference_quality_note>
Read this before comparing anything. The `stitch-extracted/` references are not uniform, and treating them as uniform will make the site worse.

**1. `DESIGN.md` is the same file five times.** `homepage/`, `products/`, `wholesale/`, `about/` and `contact/DESIGN.md` are byte-identical. It is one global system document (palette, type direction, layout principles, "Do NOT" list), not five per-page specs. There are no per-page design requirements in it to match against.

**2. Only two `code.html` files use the brand token system.** Token counts measured across the five files:

| File | `brick` | `brass` | `borderline` | `surface-container` | `outline-variant` | In brand system? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `homepage/code.html` | 79 | 7 | 65 | 0 | 0 | yes — this is what the app was ported from |
| `about/code.html` | 35 | 39 | 0 | 8 | 4 | yes |
| `products/code.html` | 1 | 1 | 0 | 42 | 83 | **no** — Material 3 tokens, different generation |
| `wholesale/code.html` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 12 | 69 | **no** |
| `contact/code.html` | 0 | 2 | 0 | 2 | 1 | **no** |

For `/products`, `/wholesale` and `/contact`, `code.html` is a different Stitch generation in an unrelated token system. **Do not port markup or classes from those three files.** Use `homepage/code.html` and `about/code.html` as the canonical component patterns, `DESIGN.md` for the palette, and those pages' `screen.png` only for section order and composition.

**3. `screen.png` files are thumbnails.** Their actual pixel dimensions: homepage 315x1600, products 457x1600, wholesale 436x1600, about 277x1600, contact 398x1600. These are downscaled full-page previews. They are usable for verifying *section order, block composition and image dominance*, and are **not** usable for verifying spacing values, font sizes or pixel alignment. Do not claim a pixel-level match against a 315px-wide thumbnail.

**4. What "matches its Stitch reference" therefore means for this phase**, and what the checkpoint will actually be judged on:
- every section present, in the reference's order;
- the asymmetric composition and photography dominance described in `DESIGN.md` is visible;
- the palette is paper/ink/muted/brick/brass/oxblood/stone/card and nothing else;
- the layout holds at 375px, 768px and 1440px with no horizontal overflow, no clipped content, no overlapping text;
- no visible defect of the three classes this phase fixed (broken image, escape text, off-palette green).

**5. Precedence when references conflict:** `REQUIREMENTS.md` > `DESIGN.md` > `homepage`/`about` `code.html` > `screen.png`. In particular `homepage/code.html` uses `bg-emerald-700` for WhatsApp CTAs; COLOR-01 overrides that and plan 01-02 already replaced it with `bg-oxblood`. Do not restore green.
</reference_quality_note>
