# Sahlok Eco Products — Direction A: Editorial Natural

## Brand Identity
Sahlok is a real Indian paper dona/pattal manufacturer based in Mirzapur, UP. The design must feel like a modern Indian manufacturing brand with connection to materials, craft, production and responsible consumption. NOT a generic ecommerce template.

## Visual Direction
Editorial manufacturing aesthetic. Think premium editorial website meets sustainable materials brand. Large photography, generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts.

## Color System
- Background: warm paper off-white #F4EFE6
- Primary text: deep warm charcoal #2B2521
- Secondary text: muted #6B5D52
- Primary CTA: terracotta brick #B24A2E
- Accent: warm brass #A87C3F
- Dark surfaces: oxblood #4A1F1C
- Borders: warm stone #C9C0B4
- Cards: white #FFFFFF
- 80% neutral/material palette with restrained accents

## Typography
- Display/Headlines: Editorial serif (Literata) at large scale
- Body/UI: IBM Plex Sans for readability
- Eyebrow labels: uppercase, tracked-out (0.14em), brass colored, 0.78rem

## Layout Principles
- 12-column editorial grid
- Asymmetric hero compositions (7/5 column splits)
- Images breaking the grid
- Generous vertical spacing between sections
- Rectangular editorial compositions with subtle radius (4px)

## Key Patterns
- Section eyebrow labels in brass
- Large photography as storytelling
- Product cards with thin stone borders, image zoom on hover
- WhatsApp as primary B2B CTA
- Scroll-reveal animations (subtle fade + translate)

## Do NOT
- Use excessive gradients or glassmorphism
- Use generic green environmental imagery
- Make it look like a Shopify template
- Use rounded pill buttons or huge radius cards
- Add unnecessary animations
